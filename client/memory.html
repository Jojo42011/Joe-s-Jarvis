The Neural Map

This is Jarvis's mind — a live picture of everything he knows about Joe and Totally Outdoors LLC. Every client, project, value, and decision is a node; every relationship is an edge. It grows after every conversation.

WHY IT MATTERS

Tools are a commodity — memory is the moat. A generic AI forgets you the moment the chat ends. Jarvis doesn't. This graph is the accumulated, private judgment of the founder: the one thing a competitor can't copy and that gets more valuable every week. When the identity bars fill in, that's the clone learning how Joe actually thinks — not just what the business does.

HOW IT FUNCTIONS

  • Six memory layers — working (the live conversation), episodic (session summaries), semantic (durable facts), relational (this graph), procedural (business rules), and self-evolving (weekly synthesis).
  • Hybrid recall — before every reply Jarvis retrieves memories by meaning, scoring each on relevance (semantic similarity), keyword match, strength, recency, and importance.
  • Reflection — periodically Jarvis reads his recent memories and writes back higher-level insights about Joe. Those conclusions become new nodes — this is how understanding forms, not just facts.
  • Decay & reinforcement — memories he uses grow stronger; memories he never touches gently fade. He forgets by not remembering, the same way people do.
  • Curiosity — after real conversations Jarvis occasionally asks a deep question across 12 psychological dimensions. Every answer deepens the identity profile below.

Nodes pulse in as they're created. Click any node to explore its connections; drag to reposition; scroll to zoom.
