What each role can reach

owner
Everything. Money, spend, integrations, the estimator, Jarvis, memory, and the ability to add or remove other people. This is Joe's role.
sales
The front of the funnel in the CRM — leads, calls, quotes, booking. Blocked from payments and milestones, from subcontractor records, and from the build-side operations. They cannot see what has been collected.
pm
The build side — stages, materials, scheduling, subs. Blocked from the estimator and cannot delete a lead. They run jobs that already exist rather than pricing new ones.
Roles are enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface — a sales sign-in that types a payments URL straight into the address bar still gets refused.
